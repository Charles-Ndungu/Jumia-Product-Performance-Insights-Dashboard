**Jumia Product Performance Analysis**



**Overview**

This repository contains a comprehensive analysis of product performance on the Jumia platform. It focuses on pricing trends, discount effectiveness, customer reviews, and ratings. The analysis is designed to help product managers, marketing teams, and data analysts quickly identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement.



**Key Features**

\- Overall Metrics: Total number of products, reviews, average rating, and average discount percentage.

\- Price Analysis: Insights on the most and least expensive products.

\- Discount Analysis: How discount levels impact customer reviews and ratings.

\- Rating Analysis: Distribution of product ratings and their correlation with reviews and discounts.

\- Interactive Dashboard: Filterable views by rating category and discount category for targeted insights.



**Purpose**

This analysis helps stakeholders:

\- Understand pricing and discount effectiveness.

\- Monitor customer satisfaction through ratings and review volumes.

\- Identify high-performing and underperforming products for strategic decisions in pricing, promotions, or product improvements.



**Technology \& Tools**

\- Microsoft Excel for data cleaning, analysis, and dashboard creation.

\- Power Query for data transformation and aggregation.

\- PivotTables and charts for visual insights.
